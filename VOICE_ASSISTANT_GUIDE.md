# Voice Assistant Guide

## How to Use the Voice Assistant

1. **Open the Voice Assistant**
   - Click the "Voice" button in the top-right corner of the application
   - The voice assistant modal will open

2. **Start Voice Recognition**
   - Click the "Speak" button or press the **Space** key
   - Your browser will ask for microphone permission (first time only)
   - Click "Allow" when prompted

3. **Speak Your Command**
   - Once listening starts, speak clearly into your microphone
   - The assistant will transcribe your speech and respond

4. **Example Commands**
   - "Mark my reading habit as done"
   - "What's my spending this month?"
   - "Add a task to review code"
   - "Show me my calendar for today"

## Troubleshooting

### Microphone Permission Denied

If you see a "Microphone permission denied" error, you need to enable microphone access:

#### Chrome / Edge
1. Click the 🔒 lock icon in the address bar
2. Click "Site settings"
3. Find "Microphone" in the permissions list
4. Change it to "Allow"
5. Refresh the page and try again

#### Safari
1. Go to Safari menu → Settings for This Website
2. Find "Microphone" in the list
3. Change it to "Allow"
4. Refresh the page and try again

#### Firefox
1. Click the 🔒 lock icon in the address bar
2. Click "Permissions"
3. Find "Use the Microphone"
4. Click "Allow"
5. Refresh the page and try again

### Voice Recognition Not Working

**Browser Compatibility:**
- ✅ Chrome (recommended)
- ✅ Edge
- ✅ Safari
- ❌ Firefox (limited support)

**Common Issues:**
1. **No microphone detected**: Check that your microphone is connected and working
2. **Permission blocked**: Follow the steps above to enable microphone access
3. **Not listening**: Make sure you clicked "Speak" or pressed Space
4. **Echo/feedback**: The assistant has built-in echo suppression, but use headphones for best results

### Tips for Best Results

1. **Speak clearly** and at a normal pace
2. **Use headphones** to prevent echo
3. **Minimize background noise**
4. **Wait for the assistant** to finish speaking before giving the next command
5. **Use the Space key** for quick start/stop of listening

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Space**: Start/Stop listening (when modal is open)
- **Escape**: Close the voice assistant modal

## Privacy

- Voice recognition is processed by your browser's built-in speech recognition API
- Audio is sent to your browser's speech service (Google for Chrome/Edge, Apple for Safari)
- Transcribed text is sent to the AI assistant for processing
- No audio is stored permanently
