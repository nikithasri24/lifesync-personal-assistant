## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### **Soft & Muted Approach**
- **Calming**: Reduces cognitive load and eye strain
- **Professional**: Sophisticated without being flashy
- **Timeless**: Won't feel dated or trendy
- **Accessible**: Better contrast ratios with softer colors
- **Versatile**: Works for all times of day

### **Color Psychology**
- **Soft Blue**: Trust, stability, calm (not aggressive)
- **Soft Lavender**: Creativity, wisdom (not loud)
- **Soft Sage**: Growth, balance (not intense)
