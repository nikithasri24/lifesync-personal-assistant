## 💡 Tips for Success

### Time Management
- **Set daily alarms**: Reminder to test
- **Block calendar**: Reserve testing time
- **Stay focused**: One module at a time
- **Take breaks**: 5 min between sessions

### Testing Efficiency
- **Follow checklists**: Don't improvise
- **Document as you go**: Don't wait until end
- **Screenshot immediately**: Don't forget later
- **Note issues but keep moving**: Don't get stuck

### Staying Motivated
- **Track progress**: Update checklist daily
- **Celebrate milestones**: 50%, 75%, 100%
- **Visual progress**: Update tracker HTML
- **Remember the goal**: Production deployment!

### Managing Issues
- **Document everything**: Even small issues
- **Categorize severity**: P0/P1/P2/P3
- **Don't fix during testing**: Document and continue
- **Review issues at end**: Prioritize fixes
